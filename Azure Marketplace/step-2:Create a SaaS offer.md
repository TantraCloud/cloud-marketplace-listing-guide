# ☁️ Create a SaaS Offer in Microsoft Partner Center

Follow these steps to create a **Software as a Service (SaaS)** offer in **Microsoft Partner Center**.

---

## 🧭 Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Sign in to Partner Center
Go to the [Microsoft Partner Center](https://partner.microsoft.com/) and **sign in** using your work account credentials.

---

### 2. Navigate to Marketplace Offers
- On the **Home page**, select the **Marketplace offers** tile.  
  *(This tile allows you to create, manage, and publish offers to Microsoft Marketplace.)*

<img width="1200" height="538" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b39a9576-351f-4ee2-8a4d-fe452da0200e" />

> Marketplace offers tile on the Partner Center Home page

---

### 3. Create a New SaaS Offer
- On the **Marketplace offers** page, select:
New offer > Software as a Service

<img width="1200" height="661" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/366e0ee7-0de1-49d9-87ff-ef96f738982b" />

> SaaS offer option displayed in the “New offer” dropdown list

---

### 4. Configure Offer Details

In the **New Software as a Service** dialog box, complete the required fields as shown below:

#### 🆔 Offer ID
- Enter an **Offer ID** value.  
This ID appears in:
- The **URL** of your Microsoft Marketplace listing  
- Any **Azure Resource Manager templates**, if applicable  
- Example:  
  If you enter `test-offer-1`, your listing URL will look like:  
  ```
  https://azuremarketplace.microsoft.com/marketplace/apps/test-offer-1
  ```

**Offer ID Rules:**
- Must be **unique** within your account  
- Maximum **50 characters**  
- Use only **lowercase letters**, **numbers**, **hyphens**, and **underscores**  
- ❌ No spaces allowed  
- ⚠️ The Offer ID **cannot be changed** after selecting **Create**

---

#### 🧾 Offer Alias
- Enter an **Offer alias** — this is the **internal name** used within Partner Center.  
- This **is not visible** in the public Microsoft Marketplace listing.  
- It’s different from the offer name that customers see.  
- ⚠️ Once created, the **Offer alias cannot be changed**.

---

#### 🏢 Associate the Offer with a Publisher
- Choose the **Publisher account** you want the offer to be created under.  
A **Publisher** represents your organization’s account.  
- If you only have one publisher account, it will be selected by default.

> 💡 **Note:**  
> The selected publisher must be **enrolled in the Microsoft Marketplace program** and  
> **cannot be changed after the offer is created.**

---

### 5. Create the Offer
After filling out all required fields:

- Click **Create** to generate your new SaaS offer.  
- Partner Center will initialize the offer setup and take you to the **Offer Overview** page, where you can continue with configuration and publishing.

---

## 🧩 Next Steps
Once your SaaS offer is created:
- Configure your **Offer setup** and **listing information**
- Add **technical configuration**, such as SaaS connection details and landing pages
- Complete the **Offer submission and certification** steps to publish to Microsoft Marketplace

For more details, refer to:  
👉 [How to create a SaaS offer in Microsoft Marketplace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/marketplace/partner-center-portal/saas-offer-setup)
