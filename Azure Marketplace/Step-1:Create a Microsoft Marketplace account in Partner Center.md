# 🏢 Create a Microsoft Marketplace Account in Partner Center

**Appropriate roles:** All Partner Center users  

To publish your offers in **Microsoft Marketplace**, you need a **Microsoft Marketplace account** in Partner Center and must ensure that your account is **enrolled in the Microsoft Marketplace program**.

This guide explains how to create a Microsoft Marketplace account and enroll in the Marketplace program.  
To verify that your account is properly enrolled, see:  
👉 [Verify your account information when you enroll in a new Partner Center program](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/).

---

## 🪧 Note

If you previously had an account in the **Cloud Partner Portal (CPP)**, your data has been **migrated to Partner Center**.  
You **don’t need to create a new account**.  
See: [Publishers who moved from the Cloud Partner Portal](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/).

---

## 🧩 Create an Account in Partner Center

Before creating a Microsoft Marketplace program account, ensure you meet the following **prerequisites**.  
Microsoft will verify this information during the account creation process.

### ✅ Prerequisites

- You must use a **work account** associated with your company or organization.  
  _Personal accounts aren’t supported._  
  See: [Company work accounts and Partner Center](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/).
- Know your **company’s legal business name**, address, and primary contact.
- You must have **authority to sign legal agreements** on behalf of your company.

There are two ways to create an account:

1. 🆕 If you’re **new to Partner Center** and don’t have a Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program account →  
   Continue to **Create a Partner Center account and enroll in Microsoft Marketplace**.
2. 🔁 If you’re **already enrolled** in Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program or Microsoft 365 & Copilot program →  
   Continue to **Use an existing Partner Center account to enroll in Microsoft Marketplace**.

---

## 🧱 Create a Partner Center Account and Enroll in Microsoft Marketplace

Use this method if you’re new to Partner Center and not enrolled in the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program.

Follow these steps to create a new Partner Center account and publisher profile.

### 1. Register on the Partner Center Enrollment Page
Review the details on the **Welcome to Microsoft Partner Center** enrollment page, then **register for an account**.

### 2. Sign in with a Work Account
Sign in using a **work account** so that your company’s email domain is linked to the new Partner Center account.  
After association, all company employees can access Partner Center using their work credentials.

📘 _To check if your company already has a work account, see:_  
[Company work accounts and Partner Center](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/).

### 3. Agree to the Terms and Conditions
As part of Microsoft Marketplace registration:
- You must accept the **Microsoft Publisher Agreement**.
- If new to the **Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program**, also accept the **Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program Agreement**.

🎉 **You’ve now created a Microsoft Marketplace account in Partner Center.**  
Continue to [Add new publishers to Microsoft Marketplace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/).

---

## 🔁 Use an Existing Partner Center Account to Enroll in Microsoft Marketplace

If you already have a Partner Center enrollment, you can use it to create your Microsoft Marketplace account.  

There are two possible scenarios:

| Scenario | Action |
|-----------|---------|
| Already enrolled in **Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program** | Use your existing Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program account to create your Marketplace account. |
| Already enrolled in **Microsoft 365 & Copilot Program** | Use your Microsoft 365 & Copilot enrollment to create your Marketplace account. |

For both, sign in to **Partner Center** with your existing credentials and ensure your **account and publisher profile** information is ready.

---

## 🪪 Use an Existing Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program Account

When you use an existing Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program account to enroll in the Marketplace, your company’s work domain is automatically linked.

You can then **assign roles and permissions** to enable access to the Microsoft Marketplace program.

### 🧭 Steps to Enroll in Microsoft Marketplace

1. **Sign in** to [Partner Center](https://partner.microsoft.com/) using your Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program account.
2. In the top-right, select **⚙️ Settings → Account settings**.
3. In the left menu, go to **Identifiers → Publisher tab**.

You’ll see two possible enrollment types:

---

### Option 1: Create a New Publisher Account

- Click **Add publisher** at the top of the publisher table.  
- Select **Microsoft Marketplace** from the list.  
- Choose the **PartnerID** to link with your publisher account and enter your company name.  
- Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program will detect your subscription and show the **Publisher profile** pane.

---

### Option 2: Enroll an Existing Publisher Account in Microsoft Marketplace

- You must have the **Owner** role in the existing publisher account.
- All publisher accounts in your Microsoft Entra tenant enrolled in Microsoft Marketplace or Microsoft 365 & Copilot will appear in the table.
- Check the **Active programs** column to see current enrollments.
- Select the **radio button** beside the publisher account you want to enroll.
- Click **Add program → Microsoft Marketplace**.
- Choose your **PartnerID** and company name.  
  _(If a PartnerID is already linked, it can’t be edited.)_
- Read and accept the **Microsoft Publisher Agreement**.
- Click **Accept and Continue** to complete enrollment.

📢 **Important:**  
To accept the terms, you must be **authorized** to act on your company’s behalf.

---

## ✅ Verify Your Registration

To confirm your account enrollment:
- Go to **Settings → Account settings → Programs** in the top-right of Partner Center.

🎉 **You’ve now successfully created a Microsoft Marketplace account in Partner Center.**  
Continue to [Add new publishers to Microsoft Marketplace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/).
