
# 💰 Pricing Models for Your Google Cloud Marketplace Product

You can choose one of the following **pricing models** for your product on **Google Cloud Marketplace**.

---

## 🆓 Free

Customers only pay for the **Google Cloud resources** that they use.  
If you are offering your product **free of charge**, skip to the section on **Submitting your pricing for review**.

---

## 💳 Subscription-Based Pricing

Customers pay a **flat monthly fee** for using your software.  
For **partial months**, the cost is **prorated** automatically.

This model is ideal for:
- SaaS platforms with predictable monthly costs  
- Applications offering tiered subscription plans (e.g., Basic, Pro, Enterprise)

---

## 📈 Usage-Based Pricing

Customers pay for your software based on **measurements (metrics)** that you define, such as data usage or storage consumption.

Examples of metrics include:
- Gibibyte-hours (GiB-hours) of storage  
- API calls per month  
- Number of active users  
- Compute time (vCPU-hours)

If you choose a **usage-based pricing model**, your app must be able to:
- **Measure** customer usage accurately  
- **Report usage data** to Google Cloud for billing and tracking

---

## 🔄 Combined Pricing

Customers pay:
1. A **base subscription fee**, and  
2. **Additional charges** based on their actual usage.

This model combines the benefits of subscription and usage pricing, ideal for:
- Enterprise apps with predictable minimum costs and variable usage
- Scalable products offering both fixed access and metered add-ons

---

## 🎁 Free Trial Option

For any pricing model you choose, you can also offer a **free trial** of your product.

This helps potential customers:
- Experience your product before committing to payment  
- Understand value and usability  
- Build trust before subscription or usage-based billing begins
