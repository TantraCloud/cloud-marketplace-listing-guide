# 🚀 Publish Your SaaS Product on Google Cloud Marketplace


This page describes the **steps to publish your SaaS product** after you have completed integration with **Google Cloud Marketplace**.

---

## ✅ Verify Your Information

Before submitting your SaaS product for review, **verify all product information** in **Producer Portal**.

🔗 **Direct link to Producer Portal:**  
[https://console.cloud.google.com/producer-portal?project=YOUR_PUBLIC_PROJECT_ID](https://console.cloud.google.com/producer-portal?project=YOUR_PUBLIC_PROJECT_ID)

After your product is launched:
- Your **Partner Engineer** creates a **public issue tracker** for your product.
- This tracker is similar to Google Cloud’s internal issue trackers and is used to manage:
  - **Bug reports**
  - **Feature requests**
  - **User feedback**

---

## 🧾 Submit Your Product for Review

To submit your product for review, you’ll use **Producer Portal**.

🔗 **Direct link to Producer Portal:**  
[https://console.cloud.google.com/producer-portal?project=YOUR_PUBLIC_PROJECT_ID](https://console.cloud.google.com/producer-portal?project=YOUR_PUBLIC_PROJECT_ID)

You can submit the following reviews to the Cloud Marketplace team **in any order**:

| **Review Type** | **Description** |
|------------------|-----------------|
| **Product Details** | Listing information, description, and marketing content |
| **Pricing** | Pricing model and payment configuration |

Once your **Pricing** review is **approved**, you must then submit:

| **Review Type** | **Description** |
|------------------|-----------------|
| **Technical Integration** | Backend, frontend, and API integration validation |

---

## ✏️ Make Changes After Submitting for Review

If you find an error or if your **Google Cloud contact** requests modifications, follow these steps:

1. **Navigate** to the page where you want to make changes.  
2. Click **Acknowledge** to confirm that any modification will require **re-submitting** the corresponding review.  
3. Make your **intended updates** to the product.  
4. **Re-submit** the affected section for **approval**.

> 💡 Tip: Minor text or metadata changes may not require full re-review, but major functional or pricing changes always do.

---

## 🌐 Publish Your Product

To publish your product on **Google Cloud Marketplace**, ensure the following:

✅ You have completed all three required reviews:
- Product Details  
- Pricing  
- Technical Integration  

✅ You are signed up as a **Google Cloud Marketplace vendor** and your account is in good standing.

---

### 📤 Steps to Publish

1. Go to your product’s **Overview page** in [Producer Portal](https://console.cloud.google.com/producer-portal?project=YOUR_PUBLIC_PROJECT_ID).  
2. Verify that all required reviews show **Approved**.  
3. Click **Publish** to make your product publicly available on **Cloud Marketplace**.

> ⚙️ After publishing, your listing becomes live for all users on [Google Cloud Marketplace](https://console.cloud.google.com/marketplace).
