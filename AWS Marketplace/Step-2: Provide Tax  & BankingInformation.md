# Provide Tax & BankingInformation
*Follow these steps to complete your AWS Marketplace seller registration. This section covers tax setup, banking details, identity verification, and next steps after submission.*

### Step 1: Provide Tax Information

Select your **country/region**.
Enter **Tax ID** (e.g., EIN for US, VAT for EU).
Complete **W-8BEN** or **W-9** form if prompted.

### Step 2: Set Up Payout (Banking) Details

Enter your **bank account** (must be business account).
Provide routing & account numbers.
Select currency (USD recommended for global reach).

AWS pays monthly via direct deposit.

### Step 3: Verify Identity & Submit

Upload **proof of business** (e.g., business license, utility bill, articles of incorporation).
Review all information.
Click **Submit Application**.

### Step 4: Wait for Approval
- You’ll receive a **confirmation email**.
- AWS reviews within **1–2 business days**.
- Once approved, you’ll get access to **Seller Dashboard**.

## After Approval: Next Steps
1. Log in to [Seller Central](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/management)
2. Create your first **product listing**
3. Choose pricing model: Pay-as-you-go, monthly, annual, etc.
4. Publish and promote!

## Common Issues & Fixes
| Issue | Solution |
|------|----------|
| "Invalid tax ID" | Double-check format; contact AWS Support |
| Bank not accepted | Use a supported country/bank [check Step-1](https://github.com/TantraCloud/cloud-marketplace-listing-guide/blob/main/AWS%20Marketplace/Step-1%3ARegister%20as%20an%20AWS%20Marketplace%20Seller.md) |
