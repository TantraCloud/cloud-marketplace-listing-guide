**Select View purchase options on the PDP.** Depending on the pricing type, either subscribe to an offer or purchase a contract.

**Subscribing**

**Avoid contract charges**  
If your product is public with high dollar contract pricing, we suggest using your seller account to create a zero-dollar private offer for the test buyer account. Your buyer account should subscribe to the zero-dollar offering to avoid being charged the contract price. For more information on Private offers, see the Private offers page in the Seller Guide.
